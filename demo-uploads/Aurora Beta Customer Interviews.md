# Aurora Beta — Customer Interview Synthesis

Date: September 28, 2026
Interviewer: Leo Varga (Product)
Participants: 8 beta households

## What people love
- Automatic scheduling of heating and EV charging; several participants said they "forgot it was running".
- Seeing the monthly savings estimate — 6 of 8 checked it weekly.

## Pain points
- Onboarding: connecting the smart meter took more than 20 minutes for 5 of 8 participants.
- Notifications feel noisy in week two, which matches the 22% week-two churn we saw in the beta.
- Participants asked for a way to invite family members to the same home.

## Implications for the launch
- A retention campaign should focus on week two: fewer notifications plus a savings recap email.
- A referral reward ("give a month, get a month") was the most requested incentive.
- Onboarding needs a guided meter-connection flow before the October 2 launch.
